Terzex Chat Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Welcome to Terzex Chat. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Terzex Chat collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our messaging service.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Terzex Chat, we may collect:

  • Account information, including your email address and user ID.
  • Profile information, such as your display name, username and profile picture.
  • Messages and media you send through the service.
  • App activity and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.
  • Online presence and delivery information needed for messaging features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Deliver messages and support conversations.
  • Display profiles and help users find one another.
  • Maintain app security and prevent misuse.
  • Fix technical problems and improve Terzex Chat.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. Messages, Photos and Videos

Messages and media are processed and stored using the services that operate Terzex Chat. They are used to provide messaging and conversation features. Access to this information should be protected through appropriate security controls.

Do not assume that messages are end-to-end encrypted. We will only describe them as end-to-end encrypted if that protection has been implemented and verified.

4. Data Storage and Firebase

Terzex Chat currently uses Google Firebase services for account authentication, database features and media storage. Information may be processed and stored on Google's infrastructure in accordance with the applicable Google service terms and privacy practices.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to:

  • Provide the messaging service and its features.
  • Operate supporting infrastructure providers such as Firebase.
  • Protect users, investigate abuse or address security issues.
  • Comply with applicable laws or valid legal requests.

6. Data Security

We work to protect information using appropriate technical and organisational measures. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their passwords and account access.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your account, request corrections, or request account and data deletion. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Terzex Chat is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Terzex Chat develops. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
